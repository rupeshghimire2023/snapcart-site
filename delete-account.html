Delete your SnapCart account

You can delete your SnapCart account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open SnapCart and sign in.
  2. Tap the menu (⋮) at the top right, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

By email

If you can't use the app, email rupeshghimire8@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my SnapCart account". We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address and password.
  • Your chosen name and your suggestion history.
  • Lists only you use, with all their items.

What is kept

  • A list you shared is handed over to the member who joined it first, so it keeps working for them. Items you added to shared lists stay on those lists, without your name.

Nothing else is kept. Deleted data may remain in our database provider's backups for up to 7 days before it is gone for good.
